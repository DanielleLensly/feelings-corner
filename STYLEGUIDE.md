# Calming Corner 🌸 • Brand & Artwork Style Guide

A comprehensive visual identity and design system guide for creating future posters, stickers, activity sheets, merchandise, and digital experiences for **Calming Corner**.

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Visual Principles

The Calming Corner visual system is designed to support **emotional co-regulation**, **sensory grounding**, and **trauma-informed safety** for children, parents, and educators.

1. **Low-Sensory & Soothing:** Avoid harsh neon colors, aggressive contrasts, or visually cluttered layouts that can overwhelm a dysregulated nervous system.
2. **Warm, Soft & Organic:** Inspired by soft watercolor washes, rounded corners, natural wooden textures, and gentle hand-drawn warmth.
3. **No Pure Black Outlines:** Replace cold `#000000` with deep plum (`#452D64`), deep forest green (`#1B4432`), or warm charcoal/espresso (`#3A4740`).
4. **Child-Centric & Accessible:** Generous font sizes, clear letterforms, friendly expressions (peaceful closed eyes `◠ ◠`, soft rosy cheeks), and tactile paths children can trace with their fingers.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

Consistent typography establishes a welcoming, playful, yet dependable tone across print and screen.

### Primary Display Font: **Fredoka**
- **Classification:** Rounded Geometric Sans-Serif
- **Google Fonts Link:** [fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka)
- **Alternative Fallbacks:** *Quicksand*, *Nunito*, *Comfortaa*
- **Characteristics:** Pillowy terminals, warm curves, open apertures, highly legible for emergent readers.
- **Recommended Usage:**
  - **Poster Main Titles (ALL CAPS):** 36pt – 48pt (Letter-spacing: `+0.04em`, Bold 700 / 800)  
    *Example:* `HOW AM I FEELING RIGHT NOW?`, `BREATHE WITH ME`
  - **Category Cards / Emotion Names (ALL CAPS):** 20pt – 28pt (Bold 700)  
    *Example:* `WALL PUSH-UPS`, `A BIG HUG`, `FIND YOUR GROUND`

### Secondary Body Font: **Quicksand**
- **Classification:** Friendly Rounded Humanist Sans-Serif
- **Google Fonts Link:** [fonts.google.com/specimen/Quicksand](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Quicksand)
- **Alternative Fallbacks:** *Nunito*, *Inter (Rounded)*, system-ui
- **Recommended Usage:**
  - **Subtitles & Directives:** 14pt – 18pt (Semi-Bold 600)  
    *Example:* *"Trace along the path with your finger"*, *"Push hard and count to 5"*
  - **Explanatory Copy & Guides:** 11pt – 13pt (Medium 500, Line-height: `1.5`)  
    *Example:* Caregiver scripts, coping tips, mindfulness steps.

### Friendly Handwritten Script (Accent / Secondary): **Patrick Hand** or **Short Stack**
- **Usage:** Child quotes, speech bubbles, informal prompts.  
  *Example:* *"Hold me please"*, *"Today I feel..."*, *"Listen to my story"*.

## 4. Logo & Monogram Guidelines

### The Official "CC" Monogram Mark
- **Concept:** The Nested / Sheltering Sanctuary ("Option D")
- **Symbolism:** An outer comforting Purple `C` (*Calming / Caregiver*) embracing a tranquil inner Green `C` (*Corner / Child / Sanctuary*).
- **Geometry Rules:**
  - Both `C`s have consistent rounded terminal stroke ends.
  - Right-hand vertical endpoints align along the same vertical axis.
  - Spacing between outer and inner curves remains uniform.
- **Clear Space:** Maintain at least `0.25 × (width)` of clear space around the mark free from text or graphics.
- **Minimum Print Size:**
  - Smallest recommended print reproduction: `15 mm` (0.6 in) wide to maintain legibility of the inner curve.

## 5. Print & Production Specifications

When sending artwork to printers or preparing sticker sheets:

1. **Resolution:** Minimum **300 DPI** at full 1:1 physical dimensions (A4, A3, or 8.5×11").
2. **Color Mode:** **CMYK** (Coated FOGRA39 or US Web Coated SWOP v2). Ensure bright watercolor pastel tones are checked against digital soft-proofing.
3. **Paper Stock Recommendation:**
   - **Posters:** 250 – 350 gsm Heavyweight Matte Fine Art Paper or Silk Cardstock. (Avoid high-gloss finishes to eliminate reflections and glare in calming spaces).
   - **Lamination:** Soft-touch matte or scratch-resistant matte laminate so dry-erase markers or small tracing fingers glide comfortably.
   - **Stickers & Badges:** Matte vinyl with waterproof backing or Cricut kiss-cut sticker paper.
4. **Bleed & Safety Margins:**
   - Bleed: `3 mm` (0.125 in) on all four edges.
   - Safe Zone: Keep critical text and illustrations at least `5 mm` inside the cut line.

## 6. Artwork Checklist for New Additions

Before approving any new Calming Corner art piece, verify:

- [ ] Does it use colors from the defined palette (Lavender, Mint, Sunshine, Rose, Sky, Terracotta, Peach)?
- [ ] Is the background a warm cream (`#FAF6F0`) rather than blinding pure `#FFFFFF`?
- [ ] Are harsh black `#000000` outlines avoided in favor of soft plum or deep forest/espresso?
- [ ] Does the font hierarchy use *Fredoka* for bold titles and *Quicksand* for gentle guidance?
- [ ] Are facial features calm and reassuring (closed smiling/peaceful eyes, soft blush)?
- [ ] Is the artwork tactile and interactive (elements children can point to, count, or trace)?
